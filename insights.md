# Retail Sales Analysis – Business Insights

## 1. Project Overview

This project uses PostgreSQL to analyse retail sales data and understand customer behaviour, sales performance, product profitability and revenue trends.

The analysis applies SQL techniques such as joins, aggregations, subqueries, CTEs and window functions to extract meaningful business insights.

## 2. Key Business Insights

### Customer Spending
Ranked customers by total spending to identify high-value customers and support loyalty programmes and targeted marketing.

### Customer Purchasing Behaviour
Identified customers purchasing across multiple categories, highlighting opportunities for cross-selling and increased customer engagement.

### Product Performance
Ranked products by revenue within their categories to identify best-selling products and support inventory planning.

### Category Profitability
Compared revenue, costs, profit and profit margins across categories to help identify profitable products and improve pricing decisions.

### Revenue Trends
Analysed monthly revenue, cumulative sales and month-over-month growth to understand business performance over time.

### Customer Retention
Identified customers who had not made a recent purchase, highlighting opportunities for customer re-engagement.

## 3. Business Recommendations

- Reward loyal and high-spending customers.
- Encourage customers to explore more product categories.
- Prioritise high-performing products when planning inventory.
- Monitor category profitability and pricing.
- Use revenue trends to improve sales planning.
- Re-engage inactive customers through targeted promotions.
